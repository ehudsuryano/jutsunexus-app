JutsuNexus

Your personal martial-arts technique library. All your videos, notes and syllabi in one place, on your phone. No account, no cloud, no ads.

What's inside

Name your techniques, notes and folders in any language. Want to try it on real content? Start from an example library.

Library

Every technique, organised in folders

Organise by art, belt, topic or anything else – folders inside folders. Each technique shows a picture from its first video, and search finds any technique by name, tag or description.

Technique

Videos, photos and notes in one place

Every technique gets its own page:

  • Videos and photos from the gallery or camera, plus documents
  • Description, execution steps, difficulty and tags
  • Your own training notes
  • YouTube links and related techniques
Playback

Play a whole folder back to back

Tap a technique's picture in the library and every video in that folder plays, one after another. Swipe to move to the next video, even into the next technique – ideal for a review before class or a belt test. In a technique's Media tab you choose the order of its videos.

Quick capture

Film at training, sort it out later

A home-screen widget opens the camera in one tap. Clips collect in the Capture inbox, and from there you add them to a technique or start a new one. Videos are compressed automatically to save space.

Sharing

Share techniques and whole folders

  • Send by link: send a short link on WhatsApp instead of a big file, and it downloads straight from your phone – how it works
  • Send by Drive link: upload to your own Google Drive and send a link that works even with your phone off – how it works

For instructors

A syllabus your students follow and update

An instructor sends the syllabus with Send by Drive link. Students subscribe from the link and get it read-only. When the instructor uploads changes the link stays the same, and students tap Check for updates to get the new version.

Works offline

Everything is stored on the phone. You only need the internet to send or open a link, or to update a syllabus.

No account

No sign-up, no server, and nobody else sees your library.

Share from YouTube

Pick JutsuNexus from YouTube's Share button and the video is saved to a technique as a link.

Saves space

In Profile you choose the video and photo quality and see how much space the library uses.

How to install

JutsuNexus isn't on the Google Play Store, so Android asks you once to allow the install. It takes about a minute. Pick where you're installing from:

  1. Download the file

    Open this site on your phone (in Chrome) and tap Download for Android.

  2. Confirm the download

    Chrome says "File might be harmful". It says this about every app file that doesn't come from the store. Tap Download anyway.

  3. Open the file

    When the download finishes, tap Open. Missed it? The file is in Chrome under ⋮ → Downloads, or in the Files app under Downloads.

  4. Let Chrome install apps

    The first time, Android blocks the install: "For your security, your phone currently isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source." Tap Settings.

  5. Turn on "Allow from this source"

    Switch on Allow from this source. On newer phones the install prompt pops up straight away; if it doesn't, tap Back.

    This only applies to Chrome, and you can switch it off again after installing.

  6. Install

    "Do you want to install this app?" – tap Install.

  7. If Google Play Protect asks

    On a phone with a Google account you'll usually get a Play Protect window: "App scan recommended". That happens to every app installed from outside the store.

    Tap More details, then Install without scanning in the part that opens. The install carries on straight away.

    Don't tap the lower blue button, Don't install app – it cancels the install.

    Samsung Galaxy

  8. Done – open it

    "App installed." – tap Open. JutsuNexus is now in your app drawer too.

Better: send the link. If you're passing the app to a friend, send them the link to this site on WhatsApp rather than the file. The link always downloads the latest version, and your friend gets the instructions too. After tapping the link, follow the From this site tab.

Screenshots in this tab are from a Samsung Galaxy. Other phones word a few buttons differently.

  1. Tap the file in the chat

    It arrives as a document whose name starts with JutsuNexus and ends in .apk. If there's a download arrow next to it, tap that first and wait for the download to finish. Then tap the file itself.

  2. Get past WhatsApp's warning

    WhatsApp shows "This file may be harmful". The big green button is Cancel – not that one. Tap Open file underneath it.

  3. Let WhatsApp install apps

    The first time, the phone blocks the install: "Permission required" for WhatsApp. Tap Settings.

  4. Switch the permission on

    On WhatsApp's Install unknown apps screen, turn the switch on (Samsung calls it Allow permission, other phones Allow from this source). The install window pops up straight away.

  5. Install

    "Install this app?" – tap Install. If Play Protect asks, tap More details → Install without scanning, as in step 7 of the From this site tab.

  6. Done – open it

    "App installed" – tap Open.

    If WhatsApp says it can't open the file: open the Files app (on Samsung: My Files), find the JutsuNexus file and tap it. The permission is then asked for the Files app instead of WhatsApp.

After installing, it's worth switching WhatsApp's permission off again, so files from other chats can't install apps. Where: Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps → WhatsApp

On newer Samsung phones the install is blocked completely by a feature called Auto Blocker. It's on by default on phones that shipped with One UI 6.1.1 or later (the S24 series and newer, for example). If you tried to install and nothing happened, or you were told the install was blocked, this is why.

  1. Find Auto Blocker

    Settings → Security and privacy, then scroll down to Auto Blocker. You can switch it off right from the switch next to it.

  2. Turn it off

    On the Auto Blocker screen the top switch should read Off. The phone asks for your fingerprint or PIN. Newer One UI versions let you turn it off for a limited time, so it switches itself back on after the install.

  3. Carry on as usual

    From here the install is the same as the From this site or WhatsApp tab. On Samsung the built-in browser is Samsung Internet, and downloaded files are in My Files under Downloads.

    Samsung's permission list: Settings → Apps → ⋮ (top) → Special access → Install unknown apps

  4. Turn Auto Blocker back on (recommended)

    After installing you can switch Auto Blocker on again and the app keeps working. To install an update later, switch it off again for a moment.

Samsung's menu names vary a little between One UI versions.

Start from an example library

Want to see how it looks before you film anything? Import an example Brazilian jiu-jitsu library: 5 techniques and 6 videos, in GI and NO GI folders. Most of the technique names are in Hebrew. You can change it or delete it afterwards.

Open the example library

  1. Install, then open it on your phone

    First install the app. Then tap the button above on your Android phone, not on a computer. You need JutsuNexus 1.4 or later.

  2. Open it in the app

    On the page that opens, tap Open in JutsuNexus. No Google account is needed.

  3. Tap Import once

    This gives you your own copy that you can edit. The download is about 170MB, so Wi-Fi is best.

    Subscribe adds it read-only instead, with a check for updates in the Nexus tab.

  4. Confirm the import

    When the download finishes, you see what's in it. Tap Import and the Brazilian jiu-jitsu folder appears in your Library. Nothing you already have gets overwritten.

What to try on it

Play a folder back to back

Open Brazilian jiu-jitsu, then GI, and tap a technique's picture. The folder's videos play one after another, and you swipe to move to the next.

Reorder videos

Open חניקת X מגארד סגור, the technique with two videos. In its Media tab tap Reorder, move them with the arrows, then tap Done.

Technique QR code

In a technique, tap the QR icon at the top. Scanning it with a camera shows the technique's details even without the app, but not its videos.

Send it to a friend

In the Library, tap ⋮ on a technique or folder and pick Send by link or Send by Drive link.

Done with the example?

It's your copy. To remove it, tap ⋮ on Brazilian jiu-jitsu and pick Delete.

Updating to a new version

There are no automatic updates. When a new version comes out, download it from this site and install it exactly like the first time – over the version you have. Instead of "Install" you'll see "Update this app?" – tap Update. All your techniques, videos and notes stay.

Don't uninstall to update. Uninstalling deletes the whole library inside the app – videos, notes, everything. Install the new version over the old one, without uninstalling.

Backups. Everything lives only on your phone. Export a backup now and then: Profile → Import / Export → Export → Entire Library and keep the ZIP file in Google Drive or on a computer. When you change phones, import that file on the new one.

Tip: send the ZIP to yourself on WhatsApp. To restore, tap it in the chat and choose to open it with JutsuNexus – the import screen opens straight away.

Sending techniques by link

Instead of a big file, send a short link on WhatsApp. The technique, with its videos, goes straight from your phone to theirs, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

  1. Pick what to send

    In the Library, tap ⋮ on a technique or folder, then Send by link.

    To send everything: Nexus → Send my whole library by link

  2. Share the link

    Tap Share link and pick WhatsApp, or any other app.

    In the same room, the other person can scan the QR code with their camera instead.

  3. Keep the send running

    You can switch apps or lock the screen, and a Sending notification stays up. Your phone has to stay online until they finish downloading.

    One link works for several people, so an instructor can send it to a whole class. When everyone has it, tap Stop sending.

  4. On the other phone: open the link

    They tap the link and then Open in JutsuNexus.

  5. Download and add it

    They see the name and size and tap Download. If the connection drops, it picks up where it stopped.

  6. Import

    When the download finishes, they see what's in it and tap Import. It's added to their library, and nothing gets overwritten.

Good to know

  • The link stops working when you stop sending. If someone opens it later, it won't connect. Send it again and share the new link.
  • Both phones need JutsuNexus 1.3 or later.
  • Anyone with the link can download it while the send is running, so only send it to people it's meant for.
  • Data and space. Without Wi-Fi it uses mobile data on both phones. While it runs, your phone needs free space of about twice the size being sent, and theirs about three times.
  • If it keeps saying "Still looking". Check the send is still running, then try switching one phone between Wi-Fi and mobile data. Some network combinations may not connect. If so, use Send by Drive link or ⋮ → Share instead.

Sending techniques by Drive link

The app uploads a copy to your own Google Drive and gives you a link. Unlike Send by link, it keeps working when your phone is off, so people can open it whenever they see it. Instructors can also update it without changing the link.

  1. Pick what to send

    In the Library, tap ⋮ on a technique or folder, then Send by Drive link.

    To send everything: Nexus → Put my whole library on Drive.

  2. Upload

    Tap Upload and get link. The first time, sign in with your Google account and allow access. If Google shows a tick box for Drive, tick it, or the upload can't start.

    The app can only see files it creates in your Drive, not the rest of it. Keep the screen open until the upload finishes.

  3. Share the link

    Tap Share link and pick WhatsApp, or any other app. In the same room, the other person can scan the QR code with their camera instead. After that, your phone doesn't need to be on.

  4. On the other phone: open the link

    They tap the link and then Open in JutsuNexus. No Google account is needed to receive.

  5. Import or subscribe

    Import once adds a copy to their library, after showing what's in it. Subscribe adds it read-only, and Check for updates in the Nexus tab gets your changes.

  6. Update or stop

    Changed something? Open Send by Drive link on it again and tap Upload latest changes. The link stays the same.

    Stop sharing deletes the file from your Drive and ends the link for everyone. What's in your library stays. Your uploads are also listed in the Nexus tab.

Good to know

  • Which one to use. Send by link uploads nothing, but your phone has to stay online until they finish. Send by Drive link takes an upload first and uses your Drive space, but works at any time.
  • Both phones need JutsuNexus 1.4 or later.
  • Anyone with the link can download it until you stop sharing, so only send it to people it's meant for.
  • Space. It uses your own Drive storage, which is 15GB free from Google. While it prepares the upload, your phone also needs free space of about twice the size being sent.
  • Busy files. Google can temporarily limit downloads of a file that many people download at once.

Adding the home-screen widget

The widget records a technique in one tap, without opening the app – made for the mat. It comes in three sizes: a record button on its own (1×1), a record button with a counter (2×1), and a wide record button with a counter (3×1).

  1. Open the widget list

    Long-press an empty spot on the home screen, then tap Widgets.

  2. Pick the JutsuNexus widget

    Search for JutsuNexus (or scroll to it), tap it to see the three sizes, pick one and tap Add. You can also long-press a widget and drag it where you want it.

    On Samsung it's the same: long-press → Widgets → JutsuNexus → pick a size → Add.

  3. The widget on your home screen

    Record opens the camera. The number next to it (clips) shows how many recordings are waiting in the Capture inbox – tap it to open the inbox.

  4. The first time: camera permission

    The first time you tap Record, the phone asks whether JutsuNexus may take pictures and record video. Choose While using the app.

  5. Record as much as you like

    After each take the camera opens again for the next one, so a whole session is one tap. When you're done, press Back.

    Your recordings wait in the Capture inbox. Open it from the widget's counter or from the bar at the top of the Library screen, and file each clip into a technique.

FAQ

Is it safe? Why does my phone warn me?

The warnings appear for every app installed from outside the Google Play Store, not just this one. The app needs no account, collects nothing and only sends what you choose to send. It only uses the internet when you send or open a link.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not at the moment – the app is Android only. Technique QR links do open on an iPhone, in the browser.

The install fails with "App not installed"

This happens when an older JutsuNexus build that was signed differently is already on the phone (for example a test version you got directly). Open the old app, export the whole library (Profile → Import / Export → Export → Entire Library), keep the file, uninstall the old app, install the new one and import the file.

Other causes: not enough free space (you need at least 250MB: the downloaded file plus the app), or an incomplete download – try downloading again.

My phone is older than Android 10

Version 1.3 needs Android 10 or later and a 64-bit phone. On an older phone, install version 1.2.2 instead. It has everything except Send by link.

I tapped the file and nothing happened

On Samsung, turn off Auto Blocker. On any other phone, open the file from the Files app, in Downloads.

How do I know there's a new version?

The latest version number is under the download button at the top of this page. The version you have is shown in the app's Profile tab.

How much space does it take?

The app itself takes about 125MB once installed. The library grows with your videos, and video compression (in Profile) shrinks them considerably.
