Privacy policy

JutsuNexus for Android. Last updated November 25, 2026. עברית

The short version

Your library stays on your phone. JutsuNexus has no accounts of its own and no server, and the developer never receives your library. Starting with version 1.9.1 (the Google Play version), the app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob; the ad service collects some device data, described below. A one-time purchase removes the ads. Apart from that, your data goes somewhere else only when you choose to send it.

Advertising

From version 1.9.1 a banner ad appears above the bottom tabs, and now and then a full-screen ad appears when you close a technique. Both are served by Google AdMob. To show ads, limit fraud and measure results, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location, derived from your IP address,
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown or tapped) and app performance and crash data.

This data goes to Google, not to the developer, and Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy (see also how Google uses data from apps that use its services). Your techniques, videos, notes and other library content are never given to the ad service.

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change that choice at any time in Profile → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Privacy → Ads). Ads in the app are limited to general-audience content.

Removing ads (in-app purchase)

You can remove all ads with a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the developer never receives your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the purchase, and remembers the answer on your phone. After a purchase the ad service is no longer started, so it no longer collects the data described above.

What stays on your phone

Your techniques, videos, photos, documents, notes and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them, so keep a backup if you need one.

When you send something

  • Send by link sends the file directly from your phone to the phones that open your link, over an encrypted connection, and nothing is stored in between. To find each other, the phones use a public peer-to-peer network, which sees a hash of the link's code and not the code or your files. The phones can see each other's IP address when connected. Anyone with the link can download the file while the send is running.
  • Send by Drive link uploads a copy to your own Google Drive and makes that file readable by anyone with the link, until you choose Stop sharing or delete it from Drive.
  • Share and Export create a file and hand it to the app you pick, such as WhatsApp or Gmail. That app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Technique links and QR codes carry the technique's text inside the link itself.
  • Opening a link downloads the package from wherever that link points, such as Google Drive or Dropbox.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

Signing in with Google is optional and only needed for Send by Drive link. The app asks for one Drive permission, drive.file, which covers only the files this app creates or opens. It can't see the rest of your Drive.

  • What the app accesses: your Google account's email address, shown on screen so you know which account is in use, and the files the app created in your Drive.
  • How it uses them: to upload, update, share and delete those files when you ask it to, and for nothing else.
  • Who receives them: your data goes directly between your phone and Google. It is not sent to the developer or to any other service (including the ad service), and it is not sold or used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Where it is kept: your sign-in stays on your phone, and the app remembers the Drive file IDs it created there. The files themselves are in your own Drive.

JutsuNexus' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Phone permissions

Camera and microphone are used to record techniques. Notifications show that a send is running. Internet access is used for the sending and link features above and, from version 1.9.1, for ads. Apart from the ad service described above, nothing is collected in the background.

Diagnostics

The app keeps a log of media processing on your phone. It leaves your phone only if you choose to share it yourself.

The websites

The download site and the web viewer are hosted on GitHub Pages and load fonts from Google Fonts, so those services may log visits. They have no analytics of their own. The viewer opens packages inside your browser without uploading them, and it doesn't send the code in a link to any server.

Removing your data

  • Use Stop sharing in the app to delete a file from your Drive, or delete it in Drive yourself.
  • Use Sign out in the app, and remove its access at your Google Account permissions.
  • Uninstall the app to delete everything stored on your phone.

Children

The app isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them. Google's own age rules apply to Google accounts.

Changes and contact

Changes are posted on this page with a new date. For questions, email ehudsuryanodev@gmail.com, or open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so don't include private details there.
